Can You Spot a Phishing Attempt?

Phishing is one of the most common ways hackers steal information. They pretend to be trusted companies to trick you into revealing personal data.

From: support@paypall.com

Subject: Urgent! Your account is locked. Click below to verify.

Body: Dear user, your account has been temporarily suspended due to unusual activity. Please click here to confirm your details immediately.

⚠️ It's FAKE!

  • Check the sender’s address — it says paypall.com with two Ls instead of one.
  • Urgent language like “Your account is locked” is used to scare you.
  • Hover over links — they don’t lead to the official PayPal domain.

From: security@google.com

Subject: New sign-in detected

Body: We noticed a login attempt from a new device. If this wasn’t you, review your account activity.

✅ This one looks REAL!

  • The sender’s domain (@google.com) is legitimate.
  • No threats or urgent language used.
  • Links direct to verified Google URLs.

🧠 Quick Tips to Avoid Phishing

  • Always double-check the sender’s email domain.
  • Don’t click suspicious links or attachments.
  • Look for spelling errors and unusual requests.
  • Never share passwords or financial info via email.
  • Enable 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication) on your accounts.
