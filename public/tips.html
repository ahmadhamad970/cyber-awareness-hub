Cybersecurity Tips & Best Practices

Simple habits can stop most cyber attacks. These tips focus on real-world threats faced by everyday users.

  • 🔐 Use unique passwords for every account.
  • 🛡️ Always enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) when available.
  • 📧 Never click links or download attachments from unknown emails.
  • 🔍 Carefully check website URLs before entering login details.
  • 🚫 Never share verification codes, even with people claiming to be support.
  • 🧠 Be suspicious of urgent messages that pressure you to act quickly.
  • 📱 Keep your operating system, browser, and apps up to date.
  • 💾 Regularly back up important data to avoid ransomware damage.
  • 🌐 Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities without a VPN.
  • 🧑‍💻 Lock your device when leaving it unattended.
  • 🔑 Use a trusted password manager instead of memorizing passwords.
  • 📨 Watch for spelling mistakes and strange domains in emails.
  • 🧪 Test suspicious links using a URL scanner before opening them.
  • 📊 Monitor account activity for unusual logins or alerts.
  • 📛 Never install software from unofficial or cracked sources.
